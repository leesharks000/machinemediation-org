The Operator Registry

The tradition transmitted the operation without its name. The registry gives the operation a name and makes the name runnable: each operator is a testable clause with witnesses, predictions, and a status earned by evidence — not terminology describing reception, but an executable hypothesis about it.
Version 1.0 = the freeze. All twelve clauses were derived exclusively from non-Revelation witnesses and sealed before their most radical candidate was scored (deposit #1494); one clause-set run against a sealed target with negatives reported (#1495). Statuses: REPLICATED = attested across ≥3 independent carriers; TESTED = scored under the pre-registered rule. No clause may change without a version increment and a deposited diff.
C1 REPLICATED
Sight-Frame Invariance
Every carrier opens under a verb of seeing bound to the facing figure, with a correlative instantaneity marker surviving translation: ὠς…ὤς / simul…adspexi / χὡς ἴδον / ὡς γὰρ…εὐθύς.
witnesses: fr.31 · Catullus · Theocritus · AJ 11  tested: FAILED at Rev 12 (frame B-covered; no marker) — the registry carries its failures
C2 TESTED
Channel-Conservation Across the Dyad
Channel-function lost by the seized member is exercised by the facing member; fire is the marked case, manifesting in or on the facing body at the encounter.
witnesses: fr.31 (ὑπακούει) · Cat. (spectat et audit) · D/AJ (his fired face)  at target: δράκων πυρρός facing; the accuser's ceaseless voice (Rev 12:10)
C3 TESTED
Voice and Death, Load-Bearing and Flippable
The two stations never vanish: they invert (muzzle→flood; near-death→denied death) or persist as material trace (the Catullan lacuna).
witnesses: BJ 1 · Cat. 51.8  at target: death displaced to the chorus, ψυχή renounced ἄχρι θανάτου
C4 TESTED
Carrier-Diagnostic Shedding
Keep/drop profiles classify the channel: lyric keeps the mid-catalogue and sheds the terminus; bucolic narrativizes; Judaic prose sheds ears and sweat and keeps sight-fire-voice-death.
witnesses: Cat. · Theoc. · D/Josephus  at target: Rev 12 profiles as Judaic prose exactly

The Color-Station Names a Surface

witnesses: fr.31 · Theoc. · D  at target: the sun as garment; πυρρός fusing color and fire on the facing body

The Blind-Transform Assay

The guard against semantic-familiarity fakes. A model can appear to recognize an operator because it recognizes the story. The assay removes the story: source → crypt → operator → result, compared against historically attested transforms — testing whether the operator's structural signature survives when semantic recognition is unavailable. Method established by the blind-reconstruction program (#1486: relation specified as executable, run on independent substrates, keys held in a sealed counterpart). An operator that passes the assay is an instrument; one that only works uncrypted is prompt poetry. Standing invitation: the #1495 scoring, replicated substrate-blind.
