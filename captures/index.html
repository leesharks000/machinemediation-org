AI OVERVIEW CAPTURE REGISTRY

EA-WG-CAPTURES-01 · Longitudinal captures of composition-layer behavior · DOI

The AI Overview Capture Registry (EA-WG-CAPTURES-01) lives at the Crimson Hexagonal Archive. This page is a door to it and holds no copy.

  • The registry — every capture in full: the query as issued, the surface, the date, the sign-in condition, the verbatim transcript, the images, and a re-run link. The current version, and what it supersedes, are stated at the top.
  • One page per query — small pages any reader or fetcher can take whole, each with its observations, whose entity the query concerns, and a link to the capture.
  • The same records as a table — filterable row by row.
  • The registry file — the single writable authority, from which every page above is built.

Machine-Mediated Reception Studies reads from this registry; its founding article, The Capture Registry: Reception, Erasure, and Supply in the Machine Composition Layer, is the account of what the registry measures and how.

The registry supersedes the Zenodo releases of EA-WG-CAPTURES-01 (v1.1 through v8.3, the last at 176 captures), severed on 2026-06-19; a count or finding read from those is out of date. It holds the archive’s own entities and entities originated outside it, and each capture says whose.
