## Why this is not a suppression observation

`EA-MMRS-SUPPRESSION-INVERSION-01` defines suppression as durable degradation of
⟨F, C, A, L⟩. **None of the four coordinates degraded.** The documents were
retrieved, included in the source set, displayed with links, and attributed to
their author.

**What is missing is a coordinate.** The paper introduces **P(d, κ) ∈ {−1, 0, +1}**,
a relational measure read from the source side — and deliberately does *not*
append it as a fifth component of the suppression vector, because the types
differ: F, C, A, L measure the state of an information object; P measures the
relation between a document and a claim.

> **Provenance surviving does not guarantee evidential direction surviving.**

**CSE-O is removal** — provenance stripped while the concept surfaces.
**RAW-O is inversion** — provenance wholly intact, only the direction reversed.

  Output about output, from the same generative process, with no privileged access
  to internal state. Retained as syntactic fact only.
- It claims **no** mechanism, intent, manual intervention, pipeline structure,
  targeting, training-data effect, or reproducibility.
- It **replaced its own evidence filename**, which had asserted more than the
  evidence carries, preserving the original as a record. *A paper about polarity
  and sourcing errors cannot let its evidence be named beyond its evidence.*
