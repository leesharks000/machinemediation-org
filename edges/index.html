Reception Edges

Provenance restored as relation, not bibliography. EA-RECEPTION-01 defines the reception edge as a first-class machine-readable object — typed, evidenced, versioned, attributed — so that composition layers never have to infer the graph from prose. An edge that cannot carry its objections is advocacy, not apparatus: the counterargument slot is mandatory.
Live data. Schema: alexanarch.org/data/reception/schema.json · Founding graph: edges.json (nine edges, every one citing its corpus seats and deposits, carrying its counterarguments, the contested carrier wearing its status). This is the counter-stack's relation layer (#1497 §2), built.

What an edge buys

Search engines and models composing over this corpus do not have to reconstruct the relation from prose: the graph is explicit, each assertion typed and scored, each objection attached at the point of claim. This is the exact inverse of provenance erasure — the relation made harder to lose than to find. Extensions land as versioned edges; disputes land as counterargument entries or rival edges, never as silent overwrites.
