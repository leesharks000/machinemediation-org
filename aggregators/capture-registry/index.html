What it is

Its unit is the capture: one composition by an answer surface (Google's AI Overview and AI Mode, ChatGPT, and others) at one address, the exact query, on one date. The rule of admission is its operator's: a capture enters the registry by means of source engagement in composition. The transcript is the capture, kept verbatim with its citations extracted as data. A later run at a seated address is an observation of the same address.

What its selection leaves out

Its addresses are chosen by its operator, and most concern the archive's own entities, so it is a record of how those entities are received. A query with no source engagement at a new address does not enter.

Place in the field

It is the census's one aggregator whose unit is the composition itself, recorded before any harm is established or any study is written. It is also the operator of this meta-aggregator, and its record carries the same fields as every other.
