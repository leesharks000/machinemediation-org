MIT AI Risk Repository

databaseharmactive
The MIT AI Risk Repository is a database of more than 1,700 AI risks extracted from 74 published risk frameworks (version 4, December 2025; the Initiative's home page gives 65+), run by the MIT AI Risk Initiative (MIT FutureTech) under CC BY 4.0. The Initiative's AI Incident Tracker, a workstream beside it, classifies AI Incident Database incidents against the same taxonomy.

What it is

Its unit is the risk as a framework states it. Each source framework is summarized, and the source documents and key excerpts are published with it. The risks are sorted into a domain taxonomy with subdomains; the April 2025 update added a subdomain for multi-agent risks.

What it gathers

It aggregates aggregators: its sources are frameworks, which are themselves syntheses. What enters is what a framework named. A risk no framework has written down has no row.

Place in the field

Its incident layer is a second-order reading of the AI Incident Database, done by machine. The repository sits over the harm basin as a taxonomy for it.
